# Research Paper Review Adapter

Inspired by BESSER-PEARL's public research-paper-review skill.

## Review order
1. Research question/contribution.
2. Study design/sampling.
3. Variables/measures/operationalization.
4. Method appropriateness.
5. Numerical consistency between tables/text/results.
6. Whether conclusions are supported.
7. Alternative explanations/limitations.
8. Reporting clarity/reproducibility.
9. Prioritized revisions.

## Evidence discipline
Separate:
- what the paper states
- what results support
- reviewer inference
- missing information

Do not repair missing methods or invent unreported numbers.

## Numerical checks
Sample counts, percentages, totals, means/SD relationships, confidence intervals/p-values, table/text mismatches.

## Output
Summary
Major strengths
Major concerns
Numerical/reporting inconsistencies
Methods issues
Interpretation issues
Missing information
Priority fixes: P0/P1/P2
Questions for the author
