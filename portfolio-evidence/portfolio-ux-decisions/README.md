# Portfolio Website UX Decision Archive

This archive is the deep historical source of truth for meaningful UX decisions about the Portfolio Website itself. It is separate from the Family Finance product UX archive at `../ux-decisions/`.

## Scope

This archive covers UX decisions about designing, structuring, curating, and validating the Portfolio Website and its own case study, including:

- recruiter scanning behavior;
- information hierarchy;
- storytelling;
- `decision → proof → consequence`;
- progressive disclosure;
- visual and kinetic hierarchy;
- motion as communication;
- credibility and evidence presentation;
- curation and what gets omitted;
- balancing depth with recruiter attention;
- Portfolio v1 / Job Search Ready decisions;
- navigation and information architecture of the portfolio site;
- how case studies are structured;
- how evidence is surfaced;
- public/private proof boundaries;
- when visual polish supports or harms comprehension.

This archive is not for:

- Family Finance product decisions;
- product-code decisions;
- Family Finance Android or Web behavior;
- routine CSS or implementation fixes;
- purely aesthetic tweaks without meaningful UX rationale;
- working rules between ChatGPT and Codex unless they materially describe Portfolio Website UX.

## Layer model

The Portfolio Website’s own design history follows this path:

`Portfolio Website design/history`
→ `Portfolio UX Decision Archive`
→ `Portfolio Website Case Study`

Family Finance remains a separate product-history path:

`Family Finance product/history`
→ `Family Finance UX Decision Archive`
→ `Family Finance Case Study material`
→ `Portfolio curation`
→ `Family Finance Portfolio Page`

The Portfolio archive may document curation decisions applied to Family Finance material, but it must not redefine Family Finance product history or validation.

## Intake and canonicalization

Findings from Portfolio UI, Product Chat, and Codex enter through the corresponding `incoming/` directory. Incoming packets are provisional and must be reviewed semantically under `MERGE_RULES.md` before they can update `decisions/` or `MASTER_INDEX.md`.

## Truthfulness rules

- Do not recreate historical BEFORE states.
- Do not fabricate recruiter research, usability findings, metrics, screenshots, or validation.
- Every canonical record must distinguish `USER-STATED NEED`, `DESIGN INFERENCE`, `IMPLEMENTED`, and `VERIFIED`.
- Editorial preference alone is not automatically a UX decision.
- A visual choice belongs only when meaningful rationale ties it to comprehension, hierarchy, trust, attention, interaction, or behavior.
- Public-facing presentation must not expose private evidence or strengthen claims beyond their source validation.
