# Incoming Decision Candidate — Single Reading Path and Limited Hierarchy

1. **Decision title:** Maintain one clear reading path with deliberately limited hierarchy.
2. **Approximate date / phase:** 2026-08-22–27; visual cognition system and narrative opening.
3. **Context / trigger:** Large/bold typography, competing asides, simultaneous visual cues, and modular sections made earlier versions feel loud or amateurish.
4. **User problem:** No scan study. `DESIGN INFERENCE`: a recruiter/viewer must know the first read, second read, and continuation path with low cognitive effort.
5. **Previous assumption/state:** Multiple large headings, metadata, color, boxes, and side content could jointly establish importance.
6. **Constraints:** Considerable content/evidence, desktop and mobile, conversational moments, warm visual identity.
7. **Alternatives considered:** Many equal-emphasis modules; poster-like headings; one main path with about three strong text levels.
8. **Rejected approaches:** Every section as a headline, five simultaneous hierarchy cues, central ideas placed as side notes, and typography as spectacle.
9. **Trade-offs:** Fewer cues create calm clarity but demand stronger content ordering.
10. **Final decision:** Use one main reading path, limited levels, medium text width, whitespace/proximity grouping, and one dominant visual surprise at a time.
11. **Resulting logic:** Importance comes from sequence, scale, spacing, and evidence timing rather than stacked decoration.
12. **Edge cases:** Metadata can remain small; asides may reward attention but cannot carry the central idea; contrast must remain readable.
13. **Viewer value:** Supports rapid orientation and deeper reading without forcing visual decoding.
14. **Portfolio/design impact:** Governs typography, section rhythm, evidence density, and first-impression hierarchy.
15. **USER-STATED NEED:** Preference for smaller/lighter type and less explanation is documented; recruiter scanning is not directly stated.
16. **DESIGN INFERENCE:** Limited hierarchy and one reading path reduce cognitive load.
17. **IMPLEMENTED:** YES in the narrative opening/current portfolio system.
