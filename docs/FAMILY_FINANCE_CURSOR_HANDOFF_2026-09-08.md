## 16. Financial safety

Never silently delete/overwrite finance data.

Key rules:

- transfers between own accounts are not income/expense
- reimbursements offset related expenses
- refund semantics must be evidence-based
- receipt/document matching must avoid amount-only matching
- ambiguity should remain ambiguity
- receipt/document channel is irrelevant when authoritative evidence exists
- queue/outbox items are removed only after accepted ingestion

Known semantic correction:

“מנהרות הכרמל” is a payment, not a refund merely because merchant text includes the Hebrew word “זיכוי”.

## 17. Receipt UX contract

Initial receipt prompt must be local-first and immediate for supported purchase events.

Prompt:

**קיבלת קבלה?**

Actions:

- `לצלם קבלה`
- `קבלה דיגיטלית`
- `אין קבלה`

Rules:

- do not wait for cloud ingestion
- do not wait for canonical creation
- do not wait for maintenance polling
- no arbitrary 30/60/90-second delay
- `קבלה דיגיטלית` = awaiting digital document, not receipt already present
- `אין קבלה` is distinct from receipt-not-required

Later missing-document logic must distinguish:

- required
- digital expected
- present
- not required
- unknown

Unknown must not immediately nag.

This is Phase 3+ work. Do not reopen before transport foundation is complete.
